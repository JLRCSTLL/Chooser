Live faces. One lucky pick.

  1. Enable your camera and get everyone in view. Numbered markers follow detected faces.
  2. Tap “Choose someone.” The highlight hops between people directly on the live video, then lands on a random winner.
  3. The camera keeps running and the winner stays highlighted. Tap “Choose again” for another round.

Keep faces visible and avoid crossing during selection. If tracking loses someone, the round is cancelled. People who enter mid-round join the next one.

Manual markers are fixed spots on live video; they don’t follow movement.
